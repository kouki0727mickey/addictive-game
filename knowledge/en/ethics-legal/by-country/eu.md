# EU — AI × Entertainment Regulation

> ⚠️ Not legal advice. → [Comparison by country](README.md) / [Cross-cutting overview (Japanese)](../../../ethics-legal/ethics-legal.md)
> Japanese edition: [../../../ethics-legal/by-country/eu.md](../../../ethics-legal/by-country/eu.md)

## Training
- Text and data mining (TDM) exception; rights holders can opt out
- **EU AI Act obligations for general-purpose AI models**: apply from August 2, 2025. Providers publish a training-data summary using the prescribed template (models placed on the market earlier have until August 2, 2027). AI Office enforcement from August 2, 2026

## Labeling & transparency (AI Act Article 50, applies from August 2, 2026)
| Provision | Content | Impact on entertainment |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 50(1) | AI that interacts directly with people must inform them it is AI (unless obvious from context) | Directly affects **conversational NPCs and AI characters** |
| Art. 50(2) | Machine-readable marking of synthetic audio, image, video, and text outputs. Systems placed on the market before August 2, 2026 have until December 2, 2026 **for this paragraph only** | Providers of generation tools and features |
| Art. 50(4) | Deployers publishing deepfakes must disclose that they are AI-generated. For **evidently artistic, creative, satirical, or fictional works**, it is enough to disclose "the existence of generated content" in a way that does not hamper enjoyment | Film and game works |

- There is no obligation to retroactively label content generated before August 2, 2026

## Personal data
- GDPR: conversation logs and memory data, age verification. In May 2025 the Italian data protection authority fined AI companion Replika €5 million (→ [case C04](../../ai-characters/case-studies.md))

## Implications for entertainment businesses
- 💡 The EU's labeling duties are the most specific. **Building in both visible labels and machine-readable marks from the start** also makes it easier to meet Chinese and Korean requirements
- Artistic works get allowances in how they are labeled, so disclosure in end credits or work information is realistic
