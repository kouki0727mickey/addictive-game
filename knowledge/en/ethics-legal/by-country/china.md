# China — AI × Entertainment Regulation

> ⚠️ Not legal advice. → [Comparison by country](README.md) / [Cross-cutting overview (Japanese)](../../../ethics-legal/ethics-legal.md)
> The original texts from Chinese authorities (CAC) have not been checked. The following is based on commentary from law firms and others (verification level B).
> Japanese edition: [../../../ethics-legal/by-country/china.md](../../../ethics-legal/by-country/china.md)

## Training
- **Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services** (promulgated July 13, 2023; effective August 15) Article 7: in training (pre-training and fine-tuning), providers must
  - use data and foundation models from lawful sources
  - not infringe others' intellectual property rights
  - obtain consent where personal information is involved
  - improve the truthfulness, accuracy, objectivity, and diversity of training data

## Voice rights
- **Civil Code Article 1023**: protection of a natural person's voice applies the portrait-right provisions mutatis mutandis. No one may distort, forge, or use another's voice without permission by means of information technology
- **Beijing Internet Court (April 23, 2024)**: China's first ruling on personality-right infringement by an AI voice. It held that the defendants' unauthorized use, in a text-to-speech app, of an AI voice built from the plaintiff's voice was infringing, and awarded a total of ¥250,000 (RMB). **Protection extends to AI-generated voices as long as they are identifiable as the person** (the same reasoning as Japan's Ministry of Justice guideline)

## Labeling AI outputs (Measures for Labeling AI-Generated Synthetic Content, effective September 1, 2025)
- AI-generated or synthesized text, images, audio, video, and virtual scenes must carry both a **visible label** (e.g., "AI-generated") and an **invisible label** such as metadata
- Visible labels are required for chatbots, AI writing, synthetic voice, face generation or swapping, and generating or editing immersive scenes
- Where download or export is offered, files must contain the label
- Malicious removal, alteration, forgery, or concealment of labels is prohibited

## Anthropomorphic AI interaction services (Interim Measures, effective July 15, 2026)
- **Ban** on "virtual partner/family" services for minors
- Guardian consent for users under 14
- Mandatory identification of minors and a minor mode (time limits, spending limits, guardian controls)
- Clear indication that it is AI
- **A usage-time pop-up after more than 2 hours of continuous use**
- Warnings when signs of excessive dependence are detected
- App stores must check security assessments and not list non-compliant apps

## Implications for entertainment businesses
- 💡 **The world's strictest regulation of AI characters and companions.** For global releases, making a minor mode and the 2-hour notice standard features makes other regions easier to handle
- Labeling can be satisfied together with EU Art. 50 and Korea's AI Basic Act through a common design (visible labels + machine-readable marks)
